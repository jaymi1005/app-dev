About the Movie

Train to Busan is a South Korean zombie movie released in 2016. It was directed by Yeon Sang-ho.

A father and his daughter board a train to Busan, but a zombie outbreak suddenly begins. The passengers must fight to survive and reach their destination.
Main Characters

Seok-woo — A father who tries to protect his daughter.

Su-an — Seok-woo's daughter.

Sang-hwa — A strong and brave passenger.

Seong-kyeong — Sang-hwa's pregnant wife.

Yong-suk — A businessman who is mainly concerned with his own survival.

My Favorite Things About the Movie

The zombie scenes are exciting.

The characters have strong emotional moments.

The action scenes are intense.

The movie shows the importance of family and sacrifice.

 Favorite Scene
